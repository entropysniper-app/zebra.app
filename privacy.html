Zebra · Privacy and data

Where your content stays, and when it leaves your device.

This note explains how the current Zebra app handles data, and what to consider when visiting the website, contacting support by email, or reporting a public issue.

Product data-handling information
Information reviewed October 17, 2026

Local by default

Notes, tags, attachments, settings, and derived search indexes are stored in Zebra's library on your device. Exports and backups go to a location you choose. The Zebra app has no account system and does not send note text to a developer server.

The current app contains no advertising, behavioral tracking, product analytics, or third-party crash-reporting service. This describes the Zebra app itself, not the separate practices of a website host, Apple, or a third-party service you choose to use.

Local search and conversion

Mac semantic search uses system Chinese and English language capabilities to process notes and queries on the device. Zebra does not download models for this feature or send note text to a model service. Notes with a password are excluded from persistent semantic indexes, even while unlocked. Keyword search remains available when system semantic capabilities are unavailable.

Mac document conversion runs locally. After you confirm an import, the converted note and retained original enter your library. Later sync or sharing depends on your settings and actions. This document conversion and semantic search are not available on iPhone or iPad.

Optional iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default for new installations; existing choices are preserved. When enabled, note text, related metadata, and referenced attachments enter your Apple Account's private CloudKit database. Notes with a password sync the same way, and Zebra does not encrypt their content. Sync also carries their password check and optional hint, never the password itself.

Apple provides iCloud and handles service data under its own terms and privacy policy. Turning sync off stops only the current device; it does not automatically erase copies in the cloud or on other devices.

Note passwords

A note with a password asks for it only in Zebra's interface; Zebra does not encrypt its content. The title, body, tags, and attachments are stored on your device in plain text like any other note and, with sync on, go to iCloud the same way. While a note is locked, the note list shows only its title, and the note is left out of search, wiki-link suggestions, and Quick Look. Notes with a password never enter Spotlight, widgets, or the semantic index, even while unlocked.

Zebra never stores the password itself, only a password check derived with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and an optional plain-text hint, which it saves and syncs. Anyone who obtains your library can use them to guess the password offline, and weak passwords may be guessed. All notes with a password share one password, which unlocks them on every device. On supported Macs, Touch ID can keep the password in that Mac's Keychain only; it does not sync to iCloud Keychain, and Zebra does not receive fingerprints or other biometric templates.

A note password keeps a note from casual view in Zebra. It does not protect the content from anyone who can read your device's library, your backups, or your iCloud data, and adding a password does not retract copies you previously exported, shared, printed, or backed up. The developer cannot recover a forgotten note password.

Web content and sharing

Clipping a page, creating a note from a URL, displaying a link card, or downloading a remote attachment may request the relevant website directly. The site receives normal connection information, such as the requested URL and IP address. The Safari clipping extension reads the current page when you invoke clipping and passes the selected content to Zebra.

Sharing, exports, Shortcuts, and other actions you authorize can provide selected content to another app or destination. That recipient handles the content under its own settings and policies.

Retention and deletion

You can delete notes and manage Trash in Zebra. Removing the app or turning sync off does not automatically remove exports, backups, or content already held by other devices or the cloud.

Deleting a note updates its current state, but older records in the cloud sync history and the attachments they reference may remain. There is currently no button to erase all cloud history. Keep an independent backup before deleting or restoring important work.

This website and issue reports

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and does not load advertising or product analytics scripts. GitHub still handles the network requests needed to serve the site, which may include an IP address, requested path, time, and browser information. GitHub's privacy statement describes its handling of this information. Local storage in the app does not mean website visits produce no network records.

When you report a public issue on GitHub, GitHub handles the account information, text, and attachments you submit, and other people can see them. We use that information to respond and investigate. Share only what is needed. Do not upload private notes, passwords, a complete library, or keys.

When you email us, we and our email provider, Gmail, process your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send to respond and investigate the issue. Share only what is needed, and do not send passwords or recovery keys.
